## B02｜MC01｜數據vs資訊

Question one. Highlight keywords: weather-station “raw readings” written to a log, “not yet” turned into charts or alerts.
Useful: raw, not yet interpreted — still data, not information.
Useless for the label: which sensors (temperature, humidity, wind) — does not change the category.
Option A. Data: uninterpreted raw facts — keep for now.
Option B. Information: processed and meaningful — cut.
Option C. Knowledge: higher organisation — cut.
Option D. Wisdom: even higher — cut.
The stem stresses “not yet interpreted”, so it is data, not information.
Therefore the correct answer is A.

## B03｜MC02｜IPO模型

Question two. Highlight: tablet “collects ballots” → server “tallies” → screen “displays” results.
Useful: the three-step order — input, process, output.
Useless: which tablet or which class election — does not change the model.
Option A. Store–output–input: wrong order and names — cut.
Option B. Input–process–output, I P O — keep.
Option C. Compress–transmit–decompress: no compression in the stem — cut.
Option D. Encrypt–backup–restore: none of that in the stem — cut.
Ballot in, tally process, display out — best match is I P O.
Therefore the correct answer is B.

## B04｜MC03｜非系統組件

## B05｜MC04｜感測器=輸入

## B06｜MC05｜資訊素養查證

## B12｜MC11｜43→2B₁₆

Option B. Two B hex: two times sixteen plus eleven = forty-three — keep.
Option C. Two D hex: two times sixteen plus thirteen = forty-five — cut.

## B13｜MC12｜位值2³

Question twelve. Highlight: binary one zero one one zero one; counting “from the right”, the “third bit whose face value is one” — what is its place value?
Useful: from the right, one-bits sit at two to the zero, two to the two, two to the three… the third is two to the three. Say place value — not “weight”.
Useless: the whole binary total — the question does not ask for the sum.
Option A. Two to the one — not the third one-bit — cut.
Option B. Two to the two — that is the second one-bit — cut.
Option C. Two to the three — keep.
Option D. Two to the four — one place further left — cut.
The third one-bit from the right has place value two to the three.
Therefore the correct answer is C.

## B14｜MC13｜−53補碼編碼

Question thirteen. Highlight: represent “minus fifty-three” in “eight-bit two's complement”.
Useful: write plus fifty-three in eight bits, then invert and add one.
Useless: I o T wording — does not change the encode steps.
Option A. Zero zero one one zero one zero one: that is plus fifty-three, not inverted — cut.
Option B. One zero one one zero one zero zero: wrong bit pattern — cut.
Option C. One one zero zero one zero one one: invert-then-add-one result — keep.
Option D. Zero one zero zero one zero one one: wrong sign bit — cut.
Fifty-three → zero zero one one zero one zero one; invert then add one → one one zero zero one zero one one.
Therefore the correct answer is C.

## B15｜MC14｜ASCII五位元組

## B16｜MC15｜偶校驗位=1

Question fifteen. Highlight: data bits one zero one one zero one one; “even parity”; what should the parity bit be?
Useful: count the ones — five, odd; even parity needs an even total, so set the parity bit to one.
Useless: transmission medium — does not change the count rule.
Option A. Set zero: five plus zero stays odd — cut.
Option B. Set one: five plus one = six, even — keep.
Option C. Cannot compute: bits are complete — cut.
Option D. Depends on medium: parity rule does not — cut.
Five ones is odd; even parity needs a one to make the total even.
Therefore the correct answer is B.

## B17｜MC16｜點陣放大鋸齒

## B20｜MC19｜96 KB單色

## B25｜B2(a)｜−78 步驟

Section B question two (a). Eight-bit two's complement reading: one zero one one zero zero one zero.
Step one: most significant bit M S B is one, so the number is negative.
Step two: invert all bits → zero one zero zero one one zero one.
Step three: add one → zero one zero zero one one one zero.
Place values: sixty-four plus eight plus four plus two equals seventy-eight.
So the decimal value is minus seventy-eight.
Do not drop the sign and write plus seventy-eight; do not invert without adding one; do not treat it as unsigned one hundred seventy-eight.

## B26｜B2(b)｜MSB符號位

Part (b).
In two's complement, the most significant bit M S B is the sign bit.
M S B equals one means the number is negative.
M S B equals zero means non-negative — positive or zero.
The sign bit takes part in the value and decides the sign.
Do not only say “it is important” without the sign meaning; do not call M S B a parity bit.

## B27｜B2(c)｜奇校驗位=1

Part (c). Data bits one zero one one zero zero one, using odd parity.
Count the ones: four — an even count.
Odd parity needs an odd total of ones including the parity bit.
So set the parity bit to one.
After that there are five ones — odd, as required.
Setting zero is the even-parity idea — wrong here.

## B28｜B3(a)｜點陣vs向量

Section B question three (a). Compare how they are built.
Bitmap: made of a pixel grid.
Vector: defined by mathematical objects — lines, curves, shapes.
Good vector examples: company logo, fonts, line art, or C A D drawings.
Reason: objects scale and edit independently; suit many output sizes.
This item does not test enlarge jaggedness — do not only talk about distortion.

## B29｜B3(b)｜取樣vs量化

Part (b). Two steps in image digitisation.
Sampling: measure or take image samples at intervals in space or time; links to resolution or sampling rate.
Quantization: map each sample’s continuous brightness or colour to a finite set of discrete levels; links to colour depth or bit depth.
Do not swap the two definitions.

## B30｜B3(c)｜≈5.93 MB

Part (c). Estimate file size.
One thousand nine hundred twenty times one thousand eighty times twenty-four, divided by eight, then divided by one thousand twenty-four squared.
First six million two hundred twenty thousand eight hundred, then divide by one million forty-eight thousand five hundred seventy-six.
About five point nine three M B.
Accept five point nine three to five point nine four.
Use one thousand twenty-four squared, not one thousand squared; do not forget divided by eight from bits to bytes.
