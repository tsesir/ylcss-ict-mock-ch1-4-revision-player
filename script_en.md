## B25｜B2(a)｜−78 步驟

Section B question two, part (a), four marks, four slots.
Eight-bit two's complement reading: one zero one one zero zero one zero.
Stage one: the most significant bit, M S B, is one, so the number is negative.
Stage two: invert all bits: zero one zero zero one one zero one.
Stage three: add one: zero one zero zero one one one zero.
Stage four: place values, sixty-four plus eight plus four plus two equals seventy-eight.
So the decimal value is minus seventy-eight.
Do not drop the sign and write plus seventy-eight; do not invert without adding one; do not read it unsigned as one hundred seventy-eight.

## B26｜B2(b)｜MSB符號位

Part (b), three marks.
Linking from (a): the M S B was one, so negative — here we explain why.
In two's complement, the most significant bit, M S B, is the sign bit.
M S B equals one: negative.
M S B equals zero: non-negative, positive or zero.
The sign bit also takes part in the value and decides the sign.
Do not just write “the M S B is important”, and do not call it a parity bit.

## B27｜B2(c)｜奇校驗位=1

Part (c), three marks.
Data bits one zero one one zero zero one, using odd parity.
Stage one: count the ones — four, an even number.
Stage two: odd parity needs an odd total of ones, including the parity bit.
Stage three: so the parity bit is set to one.
Check: with one added there are five ones — odd, as required.
Setting zero is even-parity thinking — wrong here.

## B28｜B3(a)｜點陣vs向量

Section B question three, part (a), four marks, four slots.
Two marks for how they are built, two for an example with a reason.
Bitmap: built from a grid of pixels.
Vector: defined by mathematical objects such as lines, curves and shapes.
A good vector example: a company logo, fonts, line art, or C A D drawings.
Reason: objects can be scaled and edited independently, suiting many output sizes.
Enlargement jaggedness is not tested — talking only about distortion scores nothing.

## B29｜B3(b)｜取樣vs量化

Part (b), three marks.
Sampling: measuring or taking image samples at set intervals in space or time; linked to resolution or sampling rate.
Quantization: mapping each sample’s continuous brightness or colour to a finite set of discrete levels; linked to colour depth, that is bit depth.
Key words: sampling means “samples”, quantization means “discrete levels”.
Do not swap the two definitions.

## B30｜B3(c)｜≈5.93 MB

Part (c), three marks.
Linking from (b): twenty-four bits is the colour depth from quantization.
Working: one thousand nine hundred twenty times one thousand eighty times twenty-four, divided by eight, then divided by one thousand twenty-four squared.
That gives six million two hundred twenty thousand eight hundred bytes.
Divide by one million forty-eight thousand five hundred seventy-six.
About five point nine three M B, to two decimal places.
Accept five point nine three to five point nine four.
Use one thousand twenty-four squared, not one thousand squared, which gives six point two two; and do not forget to divide by eight.
